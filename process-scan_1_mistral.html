Guardrails

Two kinds of rule. Deterministic rules can be checked by code, they either hold or they do not. Non deterministic rules need judgment, a model or a reviewer checks them. Both are sent with the scan and checked in the EVAL line of the result.
One key: value per line. Supported keys: min_words (input length), must_mention (comma separated words that must appear in the input), max_assumptions (criteria allowed to be flagged as assumption), max_rationale_words, schedule_phases (for example 4-5). Lines that are not understood are reported in the EVAL line, not silently ignored.

Effort and benefit matrix
Effort is scored inverted, so 5 means easy to build. Verdict rule: effort 4.0 or higher and benefit 4.0 or higher gives Build now. One axis at 4.0 or higher gives Waitlist. Otherwise Not now. The EVAL line reports whether the input was sufficient, the model scored on evidence and the build plan respects the guardrails. The scan cost is actual token usage times the model's list price.
