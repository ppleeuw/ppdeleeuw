HELLO.

        
I’m Peter-Paul de Leeuw. I start things and build them: companies, products, one pan-European political movement, and lately, ways for small and large organisations to actually use generative AI.

        
I believe AI will fundamentally change how we work and live. Not in the keynote way — in the quiet, practical way, where the tools get better every month until one day the work itself is different. Most organisations aren’t ready for that. Helping them is what I do: I listen to the needs, understand what the models can and can’t do, and turn both into products, launches and optimisation that hold up after I’ve left the room.

        
Offline I 3D-print things nobody asked for and play water polo — since 1994 still best described as drowning with rules.

CAREER.DOC

      

      

        
THE LONG VERSION


        
2025–2026 · BLUE HEALTH — CO-CEO

        
After handing over Amberscript I co-founded Blue Health: preventive healthcare in Amsterdam — a 360° health check in one hour, from ECG and ultrasound to bloodwork, with a doctor in the room. Huge efficiency and cost gains made possible with AI. I built the product with very happy customers, set up the tech team and made the company ready to scale.

        
The thread through everything I do is AI: getting it out of the demos and into daily work.


        
2018–2024 · AMBERSCRIPT — FOUNDER & CEO

        
During my MBA I fell for speech recognition: the models were getting good, and few people in Europe were turning them into products you could actually use. So we did. Amberscript turns audio and video into text and subtitles with proprietary foundation AI models built by our own team of 20 AI engineers and human editors where it matters.

        
From zero to €10M+ revenue in five years, profitably. A team of 100+ across the Netherlands and Germany, €15M raised from angels and VCs, two acquisitions, and a spot in the Deloitte top 10 fastest-growing companies in the Netherlands. Seven years of hiring, shipping, pricing, breaking, fixing and scaling. I handed over the keys in November 2024.


        
2017–2022 · VOLT NEDERLAND — CO-FOUNDER & CO-PRESIDENT

        
Evenings and weekends: co-founded the Dutch chapter of the pan-European political movement. We started with three people and grew it to 10.000+ members. Structured the organisation, ran the election campaigns, built the candidate lists. It probably became the first political organisation with OKRs. Volt now holds seats in both the Dutch and the European parliament.


        
2012–2017 · ACCENTURE STRATEGY — ANALYST → CONSULTANT

        
Five years advising large corporations across the Netherlands, Denmark, China and the USA: M&A pre-deal work at Philips, integrating a €2B company post-merger at ASML, growth strategies for Douwe Egberts and China Eastern, 64 digital initiatives at KLM–Air France, and putting 3D printers near oil platforms for Shell; the moment a hobby quietly became billable. Accenture taught me structured thinking, and that slides don’t ship.


        
2008–2009 · OECD — INTERNATIONAL FUTURES PROGRAMME

        
In Paris, co-wrote reports for 35 governments about what the world would look like in 2030. Forecasting the future turned out to be habit-forming.

        Erasmus University Rotterdam — MSc Economics & Business (2009–2011), cum laude, top 5%.

        Utrecht University — BSc Economics (2005–2009).
